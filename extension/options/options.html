ClientHub Tab History

Connect to ClientHub

Enter the address of the ClientHub API. You must be signed in to ClientHub in this browser; the extension uses that sign-in and never stores your password.

What is recorded

  • When tabs are opened, closed or switched, and which pages you navigate to.
  • The page address without anything after “?” or “#”, the page title, and time spent.
  • Only after you accepted the work activity notice in ClientHub. You can switch tracking off in the toolbar popup at any time.

What is never recorded

  • Page content, text you type, form fields or passwords. Private (incognito) windows. Non-web pages such as settings or local files.
